## Direct messages

A DM is a conversation between two accounts on the same instance. Every account also has a DM with itself, for notes. Opening a DM with someone creates it the first time. DMs support the same messages as channels: replies, reactions, edits, pins, files, polls and calls. Closing a DM hides it from your list until a new message or your next DM to that person reopens it.

The instance can limit who starts DMs (everyone, friends or nobody). Existing conversations keep working.

## Friends

A friend request goes to an account by name. Accepting it makes you friends, and friends see each other's online state. The friends list shows friends, incoming requests and the ones you sent. The instance can turn new friend requests off and caps friends at `FRIENDS_MAX` (1000 by default) per account.

## Who can contact you

Settings → Privacy → Who can contact you holds three choices:

| Choice                                        | Options                                                                                       |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who can DM you                                | Everyone, or friends only.                                                                    |
| Who can send you friend requests              | Everyone, people who share a server with you, or nobody.                                      |
| Who can see your profile and find you by name | Friends only, people who share a server with you, or everyone, as far as the instance allows. |

With friends only for DMs, only friends can DM you or write in a DM you already have, and you can DM only your friends. Names next to messages and in member lists always show.

## Blocking

Blocking an account (Settings → Privacy → Blocked users, or right-click the person) stops them from opening a new DM with you, writing in your DM, calling you or pinging you. A ringing or running call between you ends, and your DM with them closes. Their messages in servers can be hidden on your side. Unblocking lets DMs and pings work again from then on.

[P2P DMs](/docs/use/p2p/) go between devices and follow the P2P contact list.
